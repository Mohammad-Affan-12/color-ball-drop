# Zero-paid-marketing growth system

Success means a stable game that players understand and voluntarily return to, with measured organic discovery and responsibly limited ads. There is no promised ranking, download total, virality or income. Work moves by evidence milestones, not a calendar.

## Milestone 1 — A trustworthy first play

Finish audience/privacy decisions, device testing, final Play disclosures, sign the release bundle and pass internal/required closed testing. Invite consenting volunteers from existing contacts and appropriate Godot/puzzle communities; respect group rules and describe the build honestly. Observe actual first attempts rather than telling testers the solution. Record where they misunderstand gold supports, matching colors, cut order, free Hint and Retry. The first puzzle has an animated finger, there is no account/energy gate, and interstitial eligibility begins only after level 10 plus a session grace/frequency cap.

Exit evidence: no blocking crashes or save regressions; first-time players can explain the goal and finish level 1 with available help; background/resume, airplane mode and denied-consent play succeed on representative devices. Preserve level geometry and existing solutions unless real playtests establish a defect. Current 100-level solver tests validate solutions, not human difficulty. Treat progression pacing as a hypothesis to test.

## Milestone 2 — A credible worldwide listing

Choose all eligible intended territories and Free in Play Console manually, complete required testing and review, and use real screenshots/new icon/feature graphic. Keep the first two screenshots focused on cut → cascade → correct buckets. Start with the truthful English listing; add native-reviewed Spanish/Portuguese/other listings according to tester access and actual country demand. The six-language UI foundation is partial, so do not advertise complete legal/tutorial translation yet.

Exit evidence: owner-approved live listing, public policy/support link, content rating, accessible download on intended devices/countries, and zero broken links. Use Play's country/language acquisition and conversion reports. A larger view count alone is not proof of good conversion. Keep `STORE_LISTING.md` as the source of copy/version history.

## Milestone 3 — A repeatable puzzle-content loop

Use CONTENT.md and actual footage to make a small bank: tutorial clarity, chain-trigger prediction, decoy retry, portal payoff and mastery challenge. Keep a recognizable brand and readable first frame. Ask for a genuine proposed cut order, show the result and answer comments with useful explanations. Post clean masters manually on Shorts, TikTok and Reels; tailor the opening/caption without fake scarcity or platform watermarks. Offer an honest development post or puzzle challenge where self-promotion is permitted. A support/press contact may be prepared, but no outreach is sent without owner authorization.

Exit evidence: multiple independently posted real-game clips, a tracking sheet, working store links, and enough comparable observations to identify stronger hooks. Keep failed ideas in the sheet. Repeat the mechanic/hook that produces relevant comments, saves and qualified store visits, not the one with accidental views. There is no fixed required publishing frequency.

## Milestone 4 — Retention before higher ad pressure

After opt-in analytics is configured and validated, inspect the first-session funnel, per-level fail/retry counts and D1/D7/D30 cohorts, separated by country/build where sample size permits. Also use volunteer observations and Play crash/ANR reports. Do not optimize only for players who accepted analytics. Prefer improving unclear instructions and hint visibility over adding currencies, notifications, energy, social logins or new modes. Existing stars, best cuts, blade trails and the daily campaign selection already supply replay goals.

Exit evidence: reproducible friction points fixed, no worsening in stability/retention after changes, and a documented sample size/observation window. With small or biased cohorts, report uncertainty and continue testing rather than claiming a lift. D1/D7/D30 are measurement windows, not work deadlines. Make one meaningful change per comparison.

## Milestone 5 — Responsible monetization and regional iteration

Enable real ads only after account approval, UMP geography/withdrawal tests and actual device delivery pass. Keep free hints/retries/offline puzzles. Rewarded offers appear only when an ad is actually ready, with a clear +25-coin offer. Interstitials occur only after tapping Next on a completed level, from level 11 onward, after three unique completions and a 180-second gap/grace. No banners or app-open ads. Remove-ads entitlements from old saves are respected; the unverified purchase offer is hidden.

Review AdMob country/format impressions, fill/match/show metrics and estimated earnings. Reconcile SDK paid events; do not invent or extrapolate revenue from downloads. If ad-related reviews, failures or retention worsen, reduce exposure or disable ads while investigating. Compare regional store conversion and retention before paying with engineering time for more translation; use native reviewers for the strongest demonstrated demand. No paid mediation, campaigns, subscriptions or automatic upgrades are part of this system.

## Scorecard and guardrails

| Question | Evidence | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do visitors understand the game? | Relevant video comments + store listing conversion | Test one real screenshot/hook or description change |
| Can new players succeed? | Level 1/3/5 funnel + observed first attempts | Clarify instruction/hint; preserve validated physics |
| Do players return? | Consented installation cohorts + daily/replay behavior | Improve fairness/progression; avoid notification spam |
| Is the Android build healthy? | Actual low-end frame time/memory, Play Android vitals, crash/ANR reports | Fix stability before expanding distribution |
| Are ads worthwhile for players and owner? | Native rewarded completion, AdMob country reports, retention/reviews | Keep only tolerable exposure; never require an ad for core play |
| Are costs still zero? | Provider billing settings and usage dashboard | Maintain $0 caps; stop ingestion at free limit; no paid activation |

Owner handles all publishing, social account actions, third-party account creation, legal approval and audience declarations. No automation here publishes content, sends outreach or buys traffic/reviews.
